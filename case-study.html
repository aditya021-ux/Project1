2. Define

Persona

A

Ananya Sharma, 21

Final-year B.Com student, Shimla. Uses an Android phone for 90% of her job search.

  • Goal: land a first job in finance or operations before graduation.
  • Frustration: retyping details, silence after applying.
  • Needs: relevant listings, a reusable profile, status updates.
  • Tech comfort: high on mobile, low patience for long forms.

Problem statement

Fresh graduates need a fast, clear way to find entry-level jobs and apply once with a reusable profile, because today’s portals bury them in experience-heavy listings and leave them guessing about their application status.

How might we…

  • show only roles a fresher can realistically get?
  • make a profile and resume reusable in one tap?
  • keep applicants informed after they apply?

Findings and iterations (sample)

  • Testers missed the Apply button below the fold, so it now sticks to the bottom of the job detail.
  • Users wanted proof an application went through, so a confirmation toast and tracker entry were added.
  • Profile felt endless, so a completion meter with a checklist was added.
